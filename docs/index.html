A fast desktop calendar for your Google Calendar

Kwalendar is a Windows app that shows the calendars of all your Google accounts — personal, work, family — in one window. It opens instantly, works offline and reminds you about meetings with native Windows notifications.

Download for Windows

What Kwalendar does

All accounts in one view

Connect several Google accounts and see their calendars together in day, week, month and agenda views.

Create and edit events

Create, move, resize, edit and delete events, answer invitations, add Google Meet — the changes are saved to your Google Calendar.

Reminders that work

Windows notifications before events, with “Join” and “Snooze” buttons, plus notices when someone moves a meeting or invites you.

Instant and offline

A local copy of your calendars on your PC: the app opens immediately and keeps working without internet; edits are sent when you are back online.

Quick add

Type “Call with Peter tomorrow at 3pm for 30 min” — Kwalendar understands English and Russian.

No servers of ours

The app talks directly to Google. There is no Kwalendar account and no Kwalendar cloud.

How Kwalendar uses your Google data

  • Kwalendar asks for https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar to show your calendars and events, and to create, update, delete and respond to events when you do so in the app. It also uses openid, email and profile to show which Google account is connected.
  • Calendar data is stored only on your computer, in a local database, so the calendar opens instantly and works offline. Sign-in tokens are kept in the Windows Credential Manager.
  • Your data is never sent to the developer or to any third party, never sold, and never used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect an account in the app at any time (this deletes its local copy) and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
